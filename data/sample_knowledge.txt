The Apollo space program was conducted by NASA between 1961 and 1972. 
Its goal was to land the first humans on the Moon. Apollo 11 succeeded in this goal 
on July 20, 1969, when astronauts Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin landed the Lunar Module Eagle. 
Neil Armstrong was the first person to walk on the lunar surface, followed nineteen minutes later by Aldrin.
The Apollo program used the Saturn V rocket, which remains one of the most powerful launch vehicles ever built.
